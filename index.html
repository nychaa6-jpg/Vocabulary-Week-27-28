Add results from students' PDF files

Students who play on their own phones or computers tap DOWNLOAD RESULTS (PDF) and send the file to you (LINE, Google Classroom, e-mail…). Save the files, then choose them here. You can choose many files at once. The scores are added to the Scores tab. A file added twice is only counted once.

🔊 Voice (British English)

The game speaks with the best British English (en-GB) voice installed on this device. Each phone, tablet and computer chooses its own best voice automatically.

💡 Best British voices: iPhone / iPad – Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → Daniel or Serena (Enhanced). Windows – use Microsoft Edge (voices “Sonia”, “Libby”, “Ryan” – Natural). Android – Settings → Text-to-speech → Google → English (United Kingdom). Chrome – “Google UK English Female/Male”.
